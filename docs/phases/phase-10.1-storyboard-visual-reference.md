# Phase 10.1 — Storyboard & Visual Reference

## Objective

Lock the presentation narrative, visual references, product boundaries, and sequential delivery plan for Phase 10 before application implementation begins.

## Scope

- Finalize the five-beat story: Context, Data, Analysis, Key Insight, and So What.
- Preserve the two generated visual direction boards as development references.
- Document the visual language: warm neutral surfaces, deep navy hierarchy, restrained evidence and warning colors, generous spacing, and accessible contrast.
- Define the linear Phase 10.1–10.5 delivery sequence.
- Map every presentation beat to an existing product destination.

## Five-beat presentation map

| Beat | Audience message | Product destination | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Context | Patterns are difficult to review consistently across many schools. | Portfolio Review | Narrow from portfolio context to a school. |
| 2. Data | Outcomes, demographic groups, denominators, warnings, and separate resource context are visible together. | School Equity Profile evidence | Move from the selected school to its evidence. |
| 3. Analysis | Attendance and graduation are matched by school, year, and demographic group without hiding limitations. | Dumbbell chart and evidence details | Move from records to endpoints and limitations. |
| 4. Key Insight | A visible gap is a signal for review, not proof of cause. | Highlighted chart gap with warning context | Hold on uncertainty before deciding next steps. |
| 5. So What | A leader can document a question and assign responsible follow-up. | Authenticated Action Plan | Move from question to ownership and follow-up. |

## Required framing

> Equity Signal does not tell leaders what caused a disparity. It makes the disparity easier to see, understand, question, and act on responsibly.

Recommended presentation language:

- “The challenge is not a lack of data. It is turning scattered indicators into a reviewable question.”
- “Clarity about outcomes is not the same as certainty about causes.”
- “Where the records do not support comparison, the uncertainty stays on screen.”
- “The gap is the beginning of the conversation—not the conclusion.”
- “The product helps the right people decide what to examine next.”

## Boundaries

- Findings are observational, not causal.
- Do not introduce fixed targets, rankings, risk scores, automated recommendations, or unapproved datasets.
- Do not blend Budget / Resources context into outcome comparisons.
- Do not modify the Equity Signal logo image or its design.
- Students with disabilities and English Language Learner data remain outside this phase pending a separate data audit.

## Deliverables

- `docs/phases/phase-10-storyboard.md` as the master narrative and roadmap.
- This sub-phase plan.
- Visual reference images retained in the project root and `school-equity-resource-monitor-mockup/` development-reference folder.
- Future sub-phase plans for 10.2 through 10.5.

## Acceptance criteria

- The narrative has all five beats, with a screen or visualization mapped to each.
- The story creates constructive tension between observed evidence and uncertainty.
- The visual boards are explicitly treated as composition references, not data specifications.
- The delivery sequence is chronological and can produce one clean commit per sub-phase.
- No application, API, data, logo, or behavior change is included.
- `.env` remains ignored and excluded from all artifacts.

## Non-goals

- No UI implementation.
- No API changes.
- No new data ingestion or demographic categories.
- No Git commit until Reviewer explicitly confirms: “Phase 10.1 Complete and ready to commit.”
