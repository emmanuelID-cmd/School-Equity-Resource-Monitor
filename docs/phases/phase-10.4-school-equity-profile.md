# Phase 10.4 — School Equity Profile

## Objective

Make matched demographic outcome evidence, denominators, warnings, and uncertainty easy to understand within a school-specific profile.

## Scope

- Chart-first profile hierarchy using the existing attendance and four-year graduation comparison.
- Visible demographic records, denominators, matching status, warnings, and available school years.
- Explicit framing that a gap is a signal for closer review, not an explanation of cause.
- Responsive and accessible presentation aligned to the approved visual direction.

## Acceptance criteria

- Attendance and graduation endpoints are visually central without concealing source details.
- Matched records and data limitations remain visible and understandable.
- A profile’s year selector contains only that school’s available history.
- The product avoids causal language, fixed performance targets, rankings, and hidden risk scores.
- Budget context remains separate from outcome analysis.

## Dependency

Phase 10.3 complete and committed.

## Status

Planned.
