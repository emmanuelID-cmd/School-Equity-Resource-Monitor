# Phase 10.5 — Action Handoff & Presentation QA

## Objective

Connect a responsible review question to an authenticated Action Plan and verify the complete Phase 10 story end to end.

## Scope

- Preserve the user’s control over the review question and assignment.
- Carry school, year, and relevant visible context into Action Plans when appropriate.
- Verify the five-beat presentation path: Portfolio Review → Directory → Profile → evidence and uncertainty → Action Plan.
- Verify desktop and responsive behavior, navigation, errors, and the absence of unsupported claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Action Plan is a documentation and ownership handoff, not an automated recommendation.
- The user can review context before documenting a question or assigning follow-up.
- The complete five-beat walkthrough works without data or navigation regressions.
- No budget/outcome blending, causal claims, scores, targets, or unapproved demographic data is introduced.
- Reviewer explicitly confirms: “Phase 10.5 Complete and ready to commit.”

## Dependency

Phase 10.4 complete and committed.

## Status

Planned.
